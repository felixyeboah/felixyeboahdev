Felix Yeboah is a software engineer and designer in Accra. For ten years he has taken products from first sketch to production — React and Remix on the front, Go and Postgres underneath.

He built Reevit, Miss Cookie Spices, Waytu, Drobotix, Studio Theon, Dasheen Atelier, Dronehub, UAVOps, Blavior, The Rumson, 7even Sports Group, CSS, The Arck Interior, Desmond Weds Akyeamaa and Undisciplined.

He writes — on why he built Reevit, learning Go on real projects, mock testing with Mockery, moving to backend engineering, getting sold on Remix and 2024 in review — and takes photos.

He is taking on select projects. me@felixyeboah.dev
