About

A software developer, and a life-long learner. Over the years I’ve been building functional, beautiful interfaces and experiences that leave a positive impact on people and businesses.

How I got into tech

After high school in 2011, I wanted to further my education at university, but the financial burden was too much for my parents to bear. I had to find a way to make money to support myself.

I started learning how to code on my own in 2012 and got my first job as a software developer in 2016. Before that, I was mostly working as a freelancer. I have been working as a software developer since then.
