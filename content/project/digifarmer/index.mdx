Complete Farmer is a digital farming platform that connects stakeholders in the farming value chain. The platform employs disruptive technologies to enable users to invest in and manage farms from their smartphones. The platform also provides a marketplace for the sale of farm produce and a social network for farmers to connect and share knowledge.
DigiFarmer was one of their earliest products that enables users to invest in and manage farms from their smartphones. The platform also provides a marketplace for the sale of farm produce and a social network for farmers to connect and share knowledge.
