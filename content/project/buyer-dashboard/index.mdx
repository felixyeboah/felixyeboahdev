A platform for buyers to purchase goods/produce from farmers on the Complete farmer DigiFarmer Dashboard. CF Buyer makes dealing with let-downs from suppliers a thing of the past for you and your team. See how we make your job easier and help you crush your targets with confidence.

### Features

- **Order Management**: Buyers can place orders for produce from farmers.
- **Order Tracking**: Buyers can track the status of their orders.
- **Payment Management**: Buyers can manage their payments for orders.
- **Invoice Management**: Buyers can manage their invoices for orders.
- **User Management**: Buyers can manage their users and their permissions.
